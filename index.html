01 — Who we are

A family business that kept the country's pace.

H.E. Juma Al Majid opened his first business in 1950 — twenty-one years before the union itself, and twenty-three before the dirham replaced the Gulf rupee. What followed tracked the nation almost year for year: tyres in 1958, refrigerated food distribution in 1962, building materials in 1967, and the automotive agencies — Isuzu, Hyundai, KIA — that put a generation of Emirati families on the road.

The group is still family-led. Its capital is still largely reinvested at home. And its founder is known as widely for the Juma Al Majid Centre for Culture and Heritage — one of the Arab world's great manuscript libraries — as for anything he sold.
